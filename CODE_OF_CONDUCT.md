# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate professionally.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct,
and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.
